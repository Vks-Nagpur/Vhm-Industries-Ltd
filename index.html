Company Overview

VHM Industries Limited

VHM Industries Limited is a public limited company incorporated on 31 August 1987 and registered with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai. Over its growth journey, the company built a presence in the textile sector through cotton shirting and blended suiting and shirting fabrics, supported by a manufacturing network designed to serve domestic and international markets.

The business expanded from its original operations into additional units in Maharashtra and Gujarat, with weaving, fabric processing, and yarn dyeing capabilities added over time. Presently, the company is engaged in the manufacturing of cotton shirting, and this website functions as the formal public information and document access portal during the liquidation process.
